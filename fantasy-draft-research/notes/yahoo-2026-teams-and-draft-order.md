## Where Yahoo stores the information

Start from the desired team homepage and select **League**.

- Open the league's **Managers** page for the authoritative active-team list.
- Open **Draft Results**, then confirm the page shows **2026 draft order** rather than **2025 season draft results**.

The corresponding season-specific URL shapes are:

```text
https://football.fantasysports.yahoo.com/f1/<league-id>/teams
https://football.fantasysports.yahoo.com/f1/<league-id>/draftresults
```

Use the visible league navigation rather than hard-coding a league ID. The IDs change between leagues and seasons.
