Bottom line

  • Do not dismantle the core. Gibbs is the market's clear RB1, while Collins and London are top-20 everywhere. Flowers and Waddle preserve three-WR/flex depth.
  • Keep both tight ends for now. LaPorta has the better season rank but is returning from a hip flare-up; Andrews is healthy, on Baltimore's first team, and had the only meaningful 24-hour Sleeper add spike on this roster.
  • The weakest roster spots are not automatic drops. Michael Wilson, Jordan Mason, and RJ Harvey sit outside the top 90 on most boards, but each is a role/upside bench asset. Compare them with the live Yahoo free-agent pool before cutting anyone.
  • Stream special teams if matchup quality changes. Seattle and Cam Little are strongly rostered on Yahoo, but top-200 boards treat DST/K as replaceable endgame positions.

Week 1 controls

  • Recheck official practice status for Flowers, LaPorta, and Henderson after final injury reports.

  • Do not use Reddit sentiment to override an official designation or Yahoo availability.

Tool audit

What each surface can actually prove

The tools have different semantics. Their numbers should not be averaged into a fake consensus score.

Yahoo Fantasy

Live authority

Confirmed the current roster, lineup slots, projected season points, roster/start rates, matchups, and visible injury designations. It is the only source here that proves this team's current roster state.

DraftKick

Live, but default model

All 16 roster entries were found in the public app. Rank, projections, and VORP are useful directional checks, but the unsigned session was a ten-team default—not this eight-team custom non-PPR league.

Sleeper API

Live metadata

Confirmed identity, NFL team, active status, and the three questionable designations. Its 24-hour trend is activity, not quality; Andrews' add spike is a market-interest signal only.

FFToday

Best scoring match

The non-PPR Top 225 was updated August 30. It best matches reception scoring, but assumes a 12-team, 18-round environment and does not encode Yahoo's bonuses or eight-team replacement level.

Boris Chen

Useful, stale by design

The standard Top 200 covers every player plus K/DST and exposes tier/rank dispersion. The artifact was last modified August 28; it is consensus clustering, not a projection model.

NBC / Rotoworld

PPR fallback

The August 26 Top 200 supports a broad value cross-check. It is PPR-oriented and excludes Cam Little and Seattle from its overall 200, so it cannot be copied directly into this league.

Reddit

Discovery only

Scoped searches surfaced injury/role debates and crowd sentiment. These are labeled as low-authority context and were not used to override official team information or structured ranks.

Local Draft Assistant

Not executable

The CLI is installed in source, but no current frozen league/player board or runtime exists. Inventing inputs would defeat its deterministic safety model, so this report records the gap instead of claiming a score.
