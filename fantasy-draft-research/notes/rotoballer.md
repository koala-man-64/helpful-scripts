# RotoBaller fantasy-football source guide

Public site reviewed on 2026-08-31. This guide adds RotoBaller as a
terms-aware, visible research source for the existing Yahoo non-PPR draft
workflow. It is not an approved data feed, scraper target, or live-room
integration.

## Best use

RotoBaller is useful for a narrow second opinion on:

- overall and positional rankings in the explicitly selected scoring format;
- projections, ADP, cheat sheets, and editorial draft analysis;
- current player news, injuries, transactions, and role context; and
- preparation-only mock-draft practice.

Yahoo remains authoritative for league rules, keepers, availability, the clock,
accepted picks, and the final roster. Use RotoBaller to challenge a prepared
candidate list, not to replace those facts.

## League and evidence controls

- Start from the standard/non-PPR view, then apply the verified Yahoo scoring
  rules rather than treating the site rank as an exact custom projection.
- Remove Yahoo keepers and drafted players before comparing any external list.
- Preserve the player name, position, NFL team, URL, scoring format, visible
  update/publication label, and retrieval time. Do not join player rows by name
  alone.
- Keep rankings, projections, ADP, tiering, and editorial opinion separate.
  They have different meanings and update schedules.
- Treat a current injury, transaction, or role report as a fact to verify, not
  an automatic ranking override. Record both the RotoBaller timestamp and the
  corroborating source when it changes a decision.
- Its methodology and upstream dependencies were not inspected. Until they are,
  it is a separate editorial view but not proof of independent corroboration.

## Draft-time procedure

### Before the room opens

1. Confirm Yahoo scoring, active teams, draft order, keepers, and the player
   pool using the existing Yahoo guides.
2. Open the standard/non-PPR ranking view, projections, ADP, and any desired
   cheat sheet. Record the visible update label and retrieval time for each.
3. Build a short, league-adjusted candidate board. Remove keepers and do not
   retain or commit the underlying ranking data.
4. Use the mock-draft page only for rehearsal; do not infer Yahoo room state,
   custom scoring fidelity, or live availability from it.

### During a live pick

1. Read Yahoo first; it is the only authority for the clock and availability.
2. Use the prepared RotoBaller board as a quick tiebreaking opinion. Do not
   browse broadly or search for new tools while on the clock.
3. Open NFL player news only for a concrete injury, transaction, or role
   question between opponent picks.
4. State the scoring format, retrieval time, and one main risk when RotoBaller
   materially affects a recommendation. If the page is slow, blocked, or stale,
   fall back to the already prepared Yahoo/Boris/FFToday inputs.

## Terms, access, and reliability constraints

RotoBaller's [Terms of Use](https://www.rotoballer.com/terms-of-use) grant a
limited personal, non-commercial access license and prohibit reproducing,
republishing, storing, transmitting, or otherwise exploiting site content
outside the stated allowance. The terms also state that licensed news feeds and
data require a separate written agreement and cannot be extracted, repurposed,
or restructured without prior written consent.

Operational rule: use visible pages for personal, targeted research only. Do
not build or run a scraper, crawler, bulk exporter, background monitor,
unofficial API client, derived public player pages, or ranking-data store. Do
not bypass ads, paywalls, account controls, or tracking choices. Keep only
minimal source-attributed decision notes and obtain written permission before
any broader collection, redistribution, automation, or commercial use.

The public pages are dynamic, advertisement-heavy, and include premium upsells;
routes, scoring selectors, update labels, and available features can change.
RotoBaller expressly provides content on an as-is/as-available basis and does
not warrant that rankings, projections, or analysis are accurate, reliable,
complete, or current. Re-open the live navigation instead of constructing future
season URLs, and treat an unavailable or empty page as missing evidence.
